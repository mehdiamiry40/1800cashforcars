    How much can I get for my car in Brisbane?

    The offer depends on the make, model, year, condition, kilometres, registration status, location and how easy the car is to access for towing. Parts and recycling demand also play a part, since some makes and models are more sought after than others. Send us the details and photos and we’ll give you a clear quote — up to $9,999 for the right vehicle — before you book anything in.

    Is towing free when Aplus Car Removal buys my car?

    Free towing is usually included when Aplus buys your vehicle, as long as the location and access are suitable for our tow trucks. Driveways, front yards and street parking are generally fine. Tighter spots like underground car parks or narrow lanes may need a quick chat first so we can plan the pickup properly. We’ll always confirm towing arrangements with you before the day.

    How quickly can my car be picked up?

    Same-day or next-day pickup may be available, depending on booking time, your suburb, traffic and how accessible the vehicle is. Cars that are easy to reach, with keys and paperwork ready, tend to move through the process fastest. We’ll give you a realistic pickup window when you book, rather than a blanket promise, so you know what to expect.

    Do you buy damaged cars?

    Yes. We consider accident-damaged, hail-damaged, mechanically damaged and non-running vehicles. The extent of the damage affects the offer, but a damaged car can still have real value in parts and materials.     Do you buy unregistered cars?

    Yes, unregistered cars can usually be assessed for a quote. It helps to have proof of ownership on hand, and the vehicle should be accessible for our tow truck. Read more on our unregistered car removal page.

    Do I need a roadworthy certificate?

    For car removal or selling to a vehicle buyer, a roadworthy certificate is often not required, especially for damaged, scrap or unwanted cars. If you’re transferring registration privately or selling to another driver, the rules can be different, so it’s worth checking with Queensland Transport if you’re unsure.

    What paperwork do I need to sell my car in QLD?

    You’ll generally need proof of ownership, photo ID, your registration details if the car is still registered, and the keys if you have them. If there’s finance owing, let us know upfront. Our QLD paperwork checklist page runs through what to have ready before pickup.

    Can I sell a car that does not start?

    Yes. We regularly buy non-running cars, including vehicles with flat batteries, engine trouble, gearbox faults, overheating issues or a seized engine. Towing access still matters here, since a car that won’t start needs to be winched or loaded rather than driven onto the truck, so let us know the situation when you get your quote.
