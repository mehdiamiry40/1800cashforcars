    Is It Legal to Sell an Unregistered Car in Queensland? (The Short Answer)

    The QLD Rego Myth Busted

    Yes, it is 100% legal to sell an unregistered vehicle in Queensland.

    The confusion arises because selling a registered car privately requires a current Safety Certificate (Roadworthy Certificate or RWC). If your car is unregistered, no RWC is needed, and no official transfer of registration is required through TMR. You are simply selling a piece of property, not a registered vehicle.

    If your car’s registration has simply lapsed, you can skip this step. However, if you still have current registration but want to sell the car “as is” without an RWC, you must officially cancel the rego first.

    What to Do Why You Must Do It Aplus Car Removal’s Role
    Remove the plates. You are legally responsible for the plates until they are surrendered. We advise you on this step and wait for the plates to be removed before towing.
    Complete the TMR Cancellation Form. Lodge the ‘Vehicle/Recreational Ship Cancellation of Registration Application‘ (Form F3517). We provide you with the necessary details (like VIN) and a receipt for the sale to assist you.
    Surrender Plates to TMR. To claim any unused portion of your Compulsory Third Party (CTP) insurance refund. This guarantees you are legally separated from the vehicle and the registration liabilities.
