    What counts as a scrap car — and what we buy

    We buy a broad range of vehicles that most buyers would pass on. If your car fits any of these descriptions, it’s worth giving us a call:

    • Non-running or won’t start
    • Accident or collision damaged
    • Flood, hail, or fire damaged
    • Mechanically written off or uneconomical to repair
    • Unregistered and expired rego
    • High mileage and end of life
    • Rusted body or chassis
    • Missing parts (e.g. engine, gearbox, wheels)
    • Failed roadworthy — not worth repairing

    We accept cars, vans, utes, 4WDs, and SUVs. Make, model, and year don’t restrict us — condition does.

    How we work out a scrap car offer

    Scrap car values vary depending on several factors. Here’s what our team looks at when putting together an offer:

    • Overall condition — the more intact, the better
    • Completeness of the vehicle — missing major parts will reduce the offer
    • Catalytic converter — present or removed makes a notable difference
    • Parts demand — some makes and models have higher local demand
    • Scrap metal weight — heavier vehicles generally return more
    • Location and access — easy driveway access vs tight or remote locations
    • Towing difficulty — complex recovery situations are factored in

    For a detailed breakdown of what affects scrap car values and what you might realistically expect, see our scrap car price guide for Brisbane.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do you buy unregistered scrap cars?

    Yes. We regularly buy unregistered vehicles. You don’t need a current rego to sell your car for scrap.

    Do you take cars with accident damage?

    Yes. Collision-damaged, structurally written off, and insurance total-loss vehicles are all accepted. We assess based on what the car is worth in its current state.

    What if my car doesn’t start?

    Not a problem. We have the equipment to tow or recover non-running vehicles. Just let us know the situation when you get a quote so we can plan accordingly.

    What if I’ve lost the keys?

    We can still arrange a pickup in most cases. Mention the missing keys upfront when you call — it may affect the recovery method and, in some cases, the offer.

    Do you pick up from apartments or tight access properties?

    We handle most access situations. If your car is in an underground car park, on a narrow street, or somewhere with limited access, let us know when booking and we’ll work out the best approach.

    When do I get paid?

    Payment is made in cash at the time of pickup, before the car is loaded onto the truck.

    What affects a scrap car offer?

    The main factors are condition, completeness, make and model, scrap metal weight, and location. See our scrap car price guide for a detailed breakdown.

    Do you remove cars the same day?

    Same-day removal is available in some cases depending on location and team availability. Call us to check — we’ll give you an honest answer on timing.

    Do I need a roadworthy certificate?

    No. A roadworthy is not required when selling a scrap or end-of-life vehicle. We buy cars in any condition without one.
