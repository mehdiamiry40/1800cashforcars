    Why Eco Friendly Car Recycling Brisbane is Essential

    Old cars are packed with valuable materials, but they are also loaded with highly toxic and hazardous materials. When a car is improperly dumped or left to rust, these harmful substances can leach directly into our soil and waterways.

    The Hidden Hazards in Your Old Car

    • Toxic Fluids: Engine oil, transmission fluid, brake fluid, battery acid, and antifreeze are all highly toxic.

    • Heavy Metals: Lead (in batteries and wheel weights) and mercury (in some older switches).

    • Plastics and Rubber: Tires and interior trim materials are difficult to dispose of and release greenhouse gases if burned.

    By choosing a licensed ELV recycler like Aplus, you ensure your car does not contribute to this environmental damage.

     

    Our Step-by-Step Green Recycling Process (The 5 Rs)

    Our dismantling process follows strict Australian environmental guidelines and is meticulously designed to maximize resource recovery while eliminating environmental risk.

    1. REMOVAL (Free & Safe Transport)

    We use our specialized, licensed tow trucks to safely lift and transport your non-running vehicle to our facility. This prevents the possibility of fluid leakage on public roads or your property. We guarantee free car removal across the entire Brisbane region.

    2. RECLAMATION (The Hazardous Waste Drain)

    This is the most critical green step. Before anything else, the vehicle is prepared for fluid and material reclamation:

    • All hazardous fluids (oil, fuel, coolant) are carefully drained using specialized systems.

    • Batteries (containing lead and acid) and mercury switches are removed and sent to dedicated hazardous waste facilities for processing, not landfill.

    3. REUSE (High-Value Parts Salvage)

    This is where the high-value components come in. Our expert team identifies all reusable parts—engines, transmissions, alternators, electronics, interior trim, and body panels—and prepares them for resale or refurbishment. This component value is key to paying you the highest possible cash offer.

    4. RECYCLE (Scrap Metal Processing)

    The stripped vehicle body, consisting primarily of steel and aluminium, is crushed and bundled. These scrap metal cubes are then sent directly to metal refineries to be melted down and repurposed for new construction materials, reducing the need for virgin mining.

    5. RESPONSIBILITY (Paperwork)

    The final piece of green disposal is the legal one. We ensure all paperwork is filed, giving you proof that your vehicle has been legally and responsibly disposed of according to TMR and environmental guidelines.
