    Our Green Commitment: Beyond the Scrap Heap

    We see potential where others see junk. Aplus Car Removal is committed to a Zero-Waste Future.

    When we buy your car, it doesn’t just sit in a landfill. We rigorously follow the Environmental Protection Act 1999, ensuring that:

    • Toxic Fluids (oil, transmission fluid, coolant) are safely drained and processed.

    • Hazardous Materials (mercury switches, batteries) are removed and neutralized.

    • Scrap Metal is recycled and repurposed for new construction, reducing the need for new mining.
