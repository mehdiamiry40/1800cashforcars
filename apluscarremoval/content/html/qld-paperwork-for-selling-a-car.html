    The Four Must-Have Documents for Your Junk Car Sale

    When selling any unwanted vehicle to a licensed motor dealer or wrecker in Queensland, you need to prove two things: who you are and that the car is yours.

    Document 1: Proof of Identity (Photo ID)

    This is the non-negotiable step to prevent fraudulent or illegal sales.

    • Requirement: You must present a valid form of government-issued photographic identification.

    • Best Option: Your current Australian Driver’s Licence (QLD or interstate).

    • Acceptable Alternative: Passport, or a similar government ID (along with proof of current address, like a utility bill).

    Aplus Advantage: We require photo ID to verify your identity against the ownership documents (Document 2). This crucial step protects both you (by proving you sold the car) and us (by ensuring we comply with all QLD anti-theft and dealer licensing laws).

    Document 2: Proof of Ownership (The Title Trail)

    You need to show clear evidence that you are the legal, registered operator of the vehicle.

    • Requirement: The most recent registration certificate (even if expired), or official TMR documents listing you as the owner.

    • What if I Lost the Rego Papers? Don’t stress! If the car has been unregistered for years and the papers are long gone, you can often use a previous Transfer of Registration document, an official insurance payout statement (if it was a write-off), or contact TMR to verify ownership via the VIN.

    • Why We Need It: This allows Aplus Car Removal to legally document the vehicle’s provenance before it is dismantled or processed for salvage.

     

    Essential Post-Sale Paperwork (How to Avoid Fines)

    These documents are often the most confusing for sellers, as they determine when your legal responsibility for the vehicle ends.

    Document 3: The Notice of Disposal (NOD) or Sale Receipt

    When selling a scrap car to a licensed wrecker, the method of documenting the sale depends on whether the vehicle is still registered.

    Option A: For Unregistered Vehicles (Most Common)

    • The Solution: TMR does not require an NOD for an unregistered car. However, there are specific steps to follow to ensure you have no future liability. You can read our full guide on selling unregistered cars in Brisbane here.

    • Aplus Role: We provide a professional, legally binding contract/receipt detailing:

      • Your full name and signature.

      • The exact date and time of the sale.

      • The Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)—this is your safety net!

      • Our Motor Dealer Licence Number (Aplus Car Removal is a licensed buyer).

      • A statement confirming the car is sold “AS IS, Unregistered.”

    Option B: For Currently Registered Vehicles

    • The Solution: If the car is still registered, you must lodge a Notice of Disposal (NOD) with TMR immediately.

    • Why? This tells TMR that you are no longer the owner, preventing you from receiving tickets, tolls, or fines incurred by the new operator (or while the vehicle is being towed).

    • Our Role: We supply you with all the required buyer details (Name, ABN, Licence Number) to complete the NOD online, or we assist you with the relevant section of the rego paper.

     

    Document 4: The Plate Surrender Form (and Refund!)

    This final administrative step is purely for your financial benefit and peace of mind.

    • What to Do: If your car has QLD registration plates on it, you must remove them and hand them in at a Transport and Main Roads Customer Service Centre or an authorized agency (like a QGAP office).

    • The Refund: TMR will process a refund for any unused portion of the registration period (minus a small admin fee), which can often put a few extra dollars back in your pocket.

    • The Legal Safety Net: Surrendering the plates is the final, undeniable proof to the Queensland government that you have ended your legal association with that vehicle’s identity.

    Crucial Tip: Always obtain a receipt from TMR when you hand in your plates. Keep this document with your Bill of Sale for your records.

     

    Common Paperwork Questions Answered (The QLD Roadblocks)

    Do I need a Roadworthy Certificate (RWC) or Safety Certificate?

    No. A Roadworthy Certificate is only required when you transfer the registration of a registered vehicle to a private buyer in QLD. When you sell an old, junk, or scrap car to a licensed dealer/wrecker, you are not transferring the registration; we are purchasing the asset for wrecking or dismantling. We buy it AS IS.

    What about Outstanding Finance (PPSR)?

    If there is outstanding finance on the vehicle (a loan, lease, or chattel mortgage), it will be listed on the Personal Property Securities Register (PPSR). As a licensed buyer, we cannot legally purchase a car with clear outstanding finance.

    • Our Solution: If the value of your car is higher than the finance owed, we can help you settle the finance directly with the lender, and pay you the remaining cash difference instantly. You must inform us of any finance owed when getting your quote.

    What if I can’t be there for the pickup?

    If you are not the registered operator, or if the car is owned by an organization, we require a letter of authority signed by the owner (or company director) granting you permission to sell on their behalf, along with a copy of their ID. We still need your ID (Document 1) for the person completing the transaction.
