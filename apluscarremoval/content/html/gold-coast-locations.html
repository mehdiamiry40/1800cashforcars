    Gold Coast Local FAQs

    Can you tow a car from a Surfers Paradise high-rise basement?

    We can, but we need to know the height clearance beforehand to dispatch the correct flatbed or low-clearance truck.

    Do you buy cars with heavy salt rust?

    Absolutely. We buy cars in any condition, including those with severe structural rust that cannot pass a roadworthy test.

    How long does it take for a truck to reach the Gold Coast?

    We run trucks down the M1 daily. In most cases, we can offer same-day or next-day pickup depending on the time you book.

    Do I need a roadworthy certificate?

    No. Because we are licensed motor dealers, you can sell your car to us “As Is” without a safety certificate.

    Will you buy my old beach 4WD?

    Yes, we pay highly competitive rates for 4x4s, Utes, and commercial vehicles, even if they are heavily worn.

    Is the towing definitely free for Gold Coast suburbs?

    Yes, if we agree to buy your vehicle, the towing from your Gold Coast location is 100% free.
