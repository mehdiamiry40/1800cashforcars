    QLD Write-Offs Explained: Why Private Selling is a Nightmare

    Selling a written-off car privately in Queensland is incredibly complex and risky for both the buyer and the seller. As a licensed buyer, we navigate these rules for you, making the sale seamless.

    The Difference Between QLD Statutory and Repairable Write-Offs

    In Queensland, all major damage is recorded on the Written-Off Vehicle Register (WOVR). Your car falls into one of two official categories:

     

    1. Repairable Write-Off (RWO)

    • Definition: The repair cost exceeds the market value, but the vehicle is not structurally destroyed.

    • The Hassle: If you sell a Repairable Write-Off privately, the new owner must pay for the repairs, obtain a Safety Certificate (RWC), and pass a rigorous Written-Off Vehicle Inspection (WOVI) before TMR will allow re-registration. This takes months and thousands of dollars—which is why private buyers lowball you.

    • Our Solution: We buy your RWO car AS IS, unrepaired. We skip the inspection hassle entirely, using the car for parts and salvage value.

     

    2. Statutory Write-Off (SWO)

    • Definition: The damage is so severe (e.g., major structural collapse, extensive fire/flood damage) that the vehicle is deemed permanently unsafe.

    • The Hassle: A Statutory Write-Off can NEVER be re-registered in Australia. You can only sell it for parts and scrap.

    • Our Solution: We buy your SWO vehicle for dismantling and recycling. We guarantee a higher price than a standard scrap yard because we are equipped to safely and legally strip every valuable component and metal for reuse.
