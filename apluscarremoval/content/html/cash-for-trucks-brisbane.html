    Why Commercial Vehicles Fetch a Higher Cash Price

    Simply put: Weight equals value. Heavy vehicles are built with significantly more steel, iron, and larger components than passenger cars.

    1. Superior Scrap Base: Your average 4×4 weighs between 1.8 to 2.5 tonnes, compared to a sedan at 1.4 tonnes. This extra half-tonne or more of metal significantly boosts the base scrap valuation.

    2. Industrial Parts: Truck and 4WD transmissions, differentials, gearboxes, and turbochargers are often complex and expensive. Even when salvaged, these components command a high price on the resale market, allowing us to pay you more.

    3. Engine Size: Large, diesel, or V8 engines found in many trucks and 4x4s contain more ferrous and non-ferrous metals, increasing the value of the ‘parts’ pillar of our valuation formula.
